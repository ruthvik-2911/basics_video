SLIDE 1 — PROJECT CONTEXT: PROBLEMS & SOLUTION
============================================================================================

SLIDE TITLE: "Universal AI Knowledge Assistant"
SLIDE SUBTITLE: "Transforming Fragmented Media Archives into an Intelligent, Actionable Knowledge Base"

LAYOUT: 2 Main Columns (Left 50%: The Challenges | Right 50%: How We Overcome Them) + Bottom Impact Summary.

--- LEFT COLUMN: THE PROBLEMS WE ARE SOLVING ⚠️ ---

HEADER: "Why We Built This (The Enterprise Challenge)"

CARD 1: ⏳ Manually Scrubbing Video Timelines
- Problem: Finding a 30-second instruction inside a 45-minute training recording forces employees to manually watch and scrub through timelines, burning hours of productive time.

CARD 2: 📚 Fragmented File Silos
- Problem: Crucial knowledge is scattered across video tutorials, audio recordings, 50-page PDF reports, Word SOPs, and Excel spreadsheets with no single place to search across them all.

CARD 3: ❓ Unverifiable AI Answers
- Problem: Traditional search tools give generic text answers without proving where the information came from, leading to low trust and missed context.

--- RIGHT COLUMN: HOW OUR PROJECT OVERCOMES THEM 💡 ---

HEADER: "How Our Project Solves This (Our Solution)"

CARD 1: 🎬 Step-by-Step Visual & Timestamped Answers
- Solution: Answers video questions by displaying exact step-by-step text paired with the exact video frame screenshot and clickable timestamp (e.g. [02:30]).

CARD 2: 📄 Universal Multi-Format Brain
- Solution: Serves as a single unified search assistant for Videos (.mp4), Audios (.mp3), PDFs (.pdf), Word docs (.docx), Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx), and Images (.png, .jpg).

CARD 3: 🎯 Exact Precision Citations & Neural Voice
- Solution: Every answer is backed by verifiable citations (exact Video Title + Timestamp OR Document Page # / Excel Sheet & Row #) and can be listened to via human-like Neural Voice.

--- BOTTOM ROW: KEY BUSINESS IMPACT 🚀 ---
(3 horizontal highlight pills at the bottom)
  - ⏱️ 80% Reduction in Search Time (Instant answers instead of scrubbing videos or skimming pages)
  - 🎯 100% Verifiable Accuracy (Exact screenshot & page/row citations for every claim)
  - 🕸️ Unified Knowledge Mind-Map (Visually connects topics across all organizational files)
